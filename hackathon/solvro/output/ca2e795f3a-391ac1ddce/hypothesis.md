
# CRP-Induced Inflammatory Cascade Modulating Synoviocyte Activity

**Hypothesis ID:** 391ac1ddceeb128ba55221b28b33b7b2f74ce57b7b843b5a4172cbdc2003a74c

**Subgraph ID:** ca2e795f3a0f135cb586b88fd6d91751e0807bb3227fa468ebd3308a380e3596

We hypothesize that during acute flares of rheumatoid arthritis, elevated C-reactive protein levels trigger monocyte activation leading to a surge in pro-inflammatory cytokines. This cytokine milieu stimulates the expression of matrix metalloproteinases, which degrade extracellular matrix components that normally regulate the activity of fibroblast-like synoviocytes in the joint. Interrupting this cascade may reduce synoviocyte-mediated joint damage and provide a novel avenue for therapeutic intervention in RA.
