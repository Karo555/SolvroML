
# Bridge Therapy as a Modulator of Inflammatory Cascades in RA

**Hypothesis ID:** 84bfbbd64067d585774ef52cdb73257bc83ec6269385620c6b30c2e9019e6f7d

**Subgraph ID:** ca2e795f3a0f135cb586b88fd6d91751e0807bb3227fa468ebd3308a380e3596

We hypothesize that bridge therapy, beyond providing symptomatic relief for acute rheumatoid arthritis flares, may directly modulate the inflammatory cascade. By reducing elevated C-reactive protein levels, bridge therapy could decrease monocyte activation and the subsequent release of pro-inflammatory cytokines, leading to lower matrix metalloproteinase expression and diminished extracellular matrix degradation. This mechanism could ultimately help normalize fibroblast-like synoviocyte activity in RA joints and reduce joint damage.
