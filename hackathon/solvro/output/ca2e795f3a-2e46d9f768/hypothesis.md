
# CRP-Mediated MMP Regulation of FLS Activity in RA Flares

**Hypothesis ID:** 2e46d9f768ed2920ac81ba8e0d87522cce0f637927a5a992816edd2293e64619

**Subgraph ID:** ca2e795f3a0f135cb586b88fd6d91751e0807bb3227fa468ebd3308a380e3596

We hypothesize that in acute rheumatoid arthritis flares, elevated CRP levels trigger monocyte activation that leads to increased secretion of pro-inflammatory cytokines. This cytokine surge upregulates matrix metalloproteinases, resulting in degradation of extracellular matrix components that disrupts the regulation of fibroblast-like synoviocytes, thereby contributing to joint inflammation and subsequent tissue damage.
